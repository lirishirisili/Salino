Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026
Last updated: 13 November 2026
App name: Haserli · חסרלי
Contact: haserli@lirshir.com

1. Introduction

Haserli · חסרלי (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a real-time shared shopping list application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is stored and synced through Firebase, how household sharing works, what advertising partners may collect, and your choices. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

We do not sell your personal information.

2. Who we are

Haserli is a technology product based in Israel, founded by Liron Shiryon. For privacy questions or requests, contact haserli@lirshir.com.

3. Account and sign-in

To use cloud sync and household sharing, you create an account. You can sign in with email and password, Google, or Apple. When you use Google or Apple sign-in, we receive basic profile information from that provider (such as your name and email address) to create and maintain your Haserli account. We do not receive your Google or Apple password.

4. Shopping list and household data

List and household data are stored in Google Firebase so members of the same home can stay in sync. This may include:

  • Home profile: home name and membership
  • List items: item names, quantities, categories, checked state, and notes
  • Invite codes: short codes used to join a home
  • Preferences: language and display settings

When someone joins with your invite code, they can view and edit the shared list. You are responsible for who you invite.

5. AI features

Some features use artificial intelligence to turn your input into list items: paste a list (text you paste or share into the app), photo to list (a photo or screenshot you take, pick, or share into the app), and smart list (a short description such as “Friday dinner for 8”). Automatic category detection may also send an item name for classification.

  • The text or image is sent over an encrypted connection to our server (Cloudflare Workers), which forwards it to an AI provider (such as Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, or Cloudflare Workers AI) and returns the suggested items.
  • Photos are reduced in size on your device before upload. We do not store your photos or the text you send; our server keeps only technical logs (such as timing, item count and errors).
  • AI providers process the content to produce the result and may retain it under their own policies. Avoid sending photos or text with personal information you do not want processed.
  • You always review the suggested items before they are added to your list.

6. Device permissions

  • Camera and photos: used only when you choose “photo to list”.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: used only when you tap the microphone to add items by voice.
  • Notifications: optional; all notification types are off until you turn them on. A push token is stored to deliver the notifications you enabled.

7. Advertising

Ads keep the core app free. We use Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation with ad networks such as ironSource Ads, Unity Ads, and Meta Audience Network. Ads may appear as banners, in-list (native) ads, and full-screen ads at natural pauses — for example after a shopping trip, after adding a list, or when using an AI feature beyond the free daily use.

Advertising partners may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and usage signals under their own policies to show and measure ads. On iOS, tracking is used only if you allow it in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, and you can change it in Settings → Ad privacy. Shopping list contents are not sold or shared with advertisers.

8. In-app purchase (Remove ads)

You can buy a one-time “Remove ads” purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never receive your payment details. The app checks your purchase with the store and remembers it on your device; “Restore purchases” in Settings reads it back.

9. Analytics and on-device data

We use Firebase Analytics and basic diagnostics to understand performance and fix bugs. These process technical data such as device type, OS version, app events, and ad impression data.

Some data stays only on your device: how many free AI uses you had today, ad pacing counters, your ad-consent choice, and a local copy of your lists for offline use.

10. How we use information

  • Provide shared shopping lists, supermarket mode, and home invites
  • Turn pasted text, photos and descriptions into list items
  • Authenticate accounts and keep households in sync
  • Improve reliability, security, and product quality
  • Show ads that fund free access to the core product
  • Respond to support requests

11. Data retention and deletion

Cloud list and household data are kept while your account and home exist. You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account) or by contacting haserli@lirshir.com. We will delete or anonymize personal data unless we must retain it for legal or security reasons.

12. Your choices

You may update account details in the app, leave a home, delete your account, turn notifications on or off, change your ad-consent choice (where offered), or remove ads with a purchase. Device OS settings also control the camera, microphone, advertising identifiers and notification permissions.

13. Children

Haserli is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

14. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

15. Contact
